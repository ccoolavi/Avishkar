Operating principles

How I approach leadership and delivery.

Baseline before change

Measure the current state before redesigning it. Improvement should be defensible, not simply described.

Own the whole system

Look beyond individual tasks to process dependencies, people, data, controls and the operating environment.

Verify before trust

AI-generated or automated outputs need proportionate validation, especially when they influence business workflows.

Make adoption real

Documentation, training, feedback and operational support determine whether a solution becomes part of the business.
