How I work

Four habits that survive contact with reality.

I use AI tools heavily and I'm precise about where they help and where they don't. That distinction is the job.

01

Baseline before building

A number you didn't measure before the change isn't an outcome, it's a story. I instrument first so the delta is defensible when someone senior pushes back on it.

02

Adoption is the only metric

A system nobody uses is a hobby. I train, document, and iterate against real objections from non-technical staff until the thing is genuinely in daily use.

03

Verify what the model says

I build with AI daily. I also know exactly where it produces confident nonsense — and I design the verification step before I trust the output on anything workflow-critical.

04

Translate in both directions

Most delivery failures are definitional, not technical. Years of sitting between clients, delivery teams and engineering taught me to fix the disagreement before writing the query.
